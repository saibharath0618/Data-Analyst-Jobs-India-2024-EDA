06 — Conclusions

What this data tells us

5 actionable recommendations based on the analysis — for anyone entering or growing in the data analyst field in India.

01
Move to or target Bengaluru
380 jobs, ₹15.4L avg salary. No other city comes close for both opportunity volume and pay. If relocation is possible, this is the highest-ROI career decision.
02
Master SQL first, then Python
SQL appears in 281 listings. It is the single most required skill. Python (154) is the logical next step. Together they cover the majority of technical requirements.
03
Build Power BI dashboard skills
Power BI (133 mentions) is the #1 visualisation tool in demand. Knowing SQL + Python + Power BI makes you competitive for 80%+ of job listings in this dataset.
04
Target 2–5 yr experience roles
48.2% of all jobs are in this band. Even as a fresher, tailor your resume to appear ready for 2-yr roles by showing projects with real data and business impact.
05
Don't rely on listed salary
78% of listings don't disclose salary. Always negotiate. Use ₹9.25L (median) as your benchmark for 2–5 yrs experience. Bengaluru roles can push to ₹15L+.
